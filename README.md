# Product Case Studies

A collection of product case studies, teardowns, and PRDs, built while transitioning from software engineering into product management.

## Why this exists

I spent two years as a backend software engineer at Veritas Technologies before deciding to move into product. Rather than just saying I want to be a PM, I wanted to actually do the work, research problems, write specs, reason through trade-offs, and see if the thinking holds up. This repo is that effort: a running record of me learning to think like a PM, built on top of an engineering background rather than instead of it.

Each piece includes the full reasoning: the problem, the research behind it, the trade-offs considered, and (where relevant) the metrics used to judge success.

## Projects

### [AI Coding Mentor](./ai-coding-mentor-case-study.md)
**Type:** Product Case Study
A conversational AI tool that lets engineers ask questions about an unfamiliar codebase and get grounded, workflow-level answers instead of manually tracing code across files. Covers the problem, competitive positioning against IDE copilots, technical design decisions, and results.

### [Google Universal Cart Circles](./universal-cart-circles-prd-condensed.md)
**Type:** Product Requirements Document
A proposed feature for Google's Universal Cart that helps families (and other groups) make shared purchase decisions, with a built-in mechanism for resolving conflicting requirements without forcing a vote. [Full PRD →](./universal-cart-circles-prd-full.md)

### [Handshake Job-Matching Teardown](./handshake-teardown-case-study.md)
**Type:** Product Teardown
A breakdown of why Handshake's job search surfaces irrelevant results for both students and recruiters, tracing the issue to a specific gap in its matching pipeline, with proposed fixes.
